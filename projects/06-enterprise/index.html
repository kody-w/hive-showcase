05 / A door that opens both ways

A reversible pilot, not a deployment

PROPOSAL ONLY · HOLD
01

Bound the rehearsal

At most 10 working days or 40 labeled synthetic submissions, whichever comes first. Rehearse baseline and triage on paired inputs; keep the pooled pod as a stress comparator. No production access or policy decisions.

02

Measure both sides

Record every arrival, queue, return, and handoff. Compare clean and exception tails, incomplete-data holds, packet rework, and reviewer comprehension—not just average lead time. Real lead time and savings remain unobserved.

03

Stop, preserve, revert

Stop on any lost case, altered original, false approval, or capacity violation. Also stop after two paired batches with >10% worse p95 wait or >15% worse clean-tail time. Keep the audit ledger; reset to baseline and cease the offline exercise.

There is no production migration to roll back. Source corrections, policy ownership, adoption, enterprise access, and any later live pilot require separate explicit approvals. This model grants none.
