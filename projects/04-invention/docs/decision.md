## What survived falsification

All 12 authored reference counts match. All 504 assignments preserve exactly
one occurrence of each instance and both integer capacities. Beam-128 wins
the frozen candidate-selection rule: 15 vs 16 authored bins and 300 vs 323
paired bins, with 23 paired wins, 57 ties and no losses. The complete trial
set—not a favorable permutation—determines the result.

The selected counts are scalar-optimal on these inputs: aggregate bounds
certify order-trap, weight-trap and mixed-classroom; bounded exhaustive
search certifies bulky-gaps. This cross-method evidence does not turn the
beam algorithm into an exact solver for arbitrary inputs.

## What failed or remains uncertain

- Dual-capacity best-fit ties the authored total and regresses on
  mixed-classroom. Its 7-bin result is worse than input-order's 6. Reject it
  under the frozen gate even though its paired grand total is slightly lower.
- Dominant sorting reproduces the known mixed-classroom regression;
  volume-first is worse still (8). In weight-trap, filling light bulky totes
  with medium boards strands the dense pouches and uses five instead of four.
- Bulky-gaps disproves the shortcut “aggregate lower bound equals answer.”
  Indivisibility forces three bins rather than two.
- Bounded exact declines 15 fourteen-item searches instead of pretending its
  first-fit incumbents are optimal. No node limit was reached in this study;
  separate tests verify that a forced cap remains explicitly unproven.
- The experiment is four authored synthetic cases, not representative
  library inventory, an independent replication, or measured physical work.

## Bounded application hypothesis

A potential user job is inspecting a small kit-grouping plan and correcting
it without surrendering data to a service. A local printable worksheet with
visible constraints is the lower-complexity hypothesis. An account-based
integration service would add data collection, support and procurement
questions; this work supplies no evidence that those costs are warranted.

Disconfirming signals include geometry dominating the count optimization,
unclear labels, unreliable item data, manual work being simpler, or no
repeated planning problem. Any of those is a reason to stop or simplify.
No external interviews, positioning, purchases, publications or integrations
have been authorized or performed.

All three candidate families are established algorithmic ideas implemented
originally here. No literature/patent novelty review, patentability claim,
freedom-to-operate assessment, customer validation or savings claim follows
from a smaller synthetic bin count.
