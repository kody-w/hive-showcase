    LOWER BOUND ≠ ANSWER
    Three items. A bound of two. Three totes.
    In bulky-gaps, every item has volume 6. Any pair totals 12, beyond the capacity of 10. The aggregate bound ceil(18/10) = 2 is unattainable.
    Bounded exhaustive search proves three for this scalar case. A beam or greedy count above the aggregate bound is not its own proof.
