How the score works

C × (wL × L/5 + wR × R/5 + wS × S) / (wL + wR + wS)

C = subjective confidence, 0–100. L and R = ordinal learning/reuse, 1–5. S = max(0, (300 − delivery − test preparation − review) / 300). In seed-duration mode, the original hours replace delivery + preparation.

Scores are dimensionless heuristics, not probabilities or dollars. Ties within 10⁻⁹ prefer fewer minutes, then the lexical venture ID. All-zero weights are invalid; all-zero scores recommend nothing.
