            What does “smallest” mean?
            
    First minimize the number of changed fields. Then minimize the sum of minutes changed. A cutoff is one field; each task duration is another. A 1-minute step means 1-minute candidates, not continuous time.
    
            
    Only cutoff extensions up to your limit and task reductions down to your floors are considered. Windows, dependencies, fixed events and task membership never change. All cheaper candidates must be proven infeasible before a minimum is claimed.
